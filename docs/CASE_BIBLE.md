# Case 002 — Dead Air (Phase 2; internal spoilers)

## Premise and truth

Breakwater Radio, 18 February 2026. A false harbor-closure bulletin airs at 22:14 during a fundraising broadcast. The public ferry's 22:20 trial departure is paused and canceled under its safety rules; no passengers are harmed. The channel was open. Station records, cartridge and financial documents establish deliberate interference.

Celia Ward (S13), director of rival Cedarline Ferries, inserts a prepared cartridge into the unattended reserve queue at **22:06**, eight minutes before the bulletin airs. The station queue terminal is seven minutes fast and prints **22:13**. The on-air clock is correct. Her telephone call at 22:10–22:18 is a genuine alibi for the broadcast, but not for the earlier insertion. Cedarline receives an £80,000 standby credit and first consideration of a berth lease if the public trial is canceled, regardless of cause. Celia's written assurance misleadingly says only a harbor-master closure could trigger payment.
